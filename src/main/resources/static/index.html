How the check works

  1. 1

    Read the strip

    NVIDIA Nemotron reads the medicine name, batch number and expiry date from your photo.

  2. 2

    Match government alerts

    The batch is matched against every batch CDSCO has reported as failing a lab test or as spurious, and checked for newer alerts online.

  3. 3

    Explain what to do

    You get a clear answer with the official source, safe next steps, and a message you can send to family on WhatsApp.
